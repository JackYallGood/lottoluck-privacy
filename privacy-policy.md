
Privacy Policy
Effective Date: January 2025

Lotto Luck (“the App”) is created by Captain Jack Apps. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains what information we do and do not collect, how ads function inside the app, and your rights as a user.

1. Information We Collect

We do NOT collect, store, or transmit any personal information.
Lotto Luck does not request your name, phone number, email address, location, or any other personal data.

We do not:

Create user accounts

Use analytics

Track behavior

Store numbers you enter

All number selections stay on your device and are not transmitted anywhere.

2. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

Lotto Luck displays Google AdMob ads, which may collect device-level identifiers for the purpose of delivering and measuring ads.

Examples of data that Google AdMob may automatically collect include:

Device identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID)

IP address (may be used for coarse location)

App interactions related to ad display

Approximate region (non-precise)

We do not receive or store this information.
All advertising data is handled by Google under their own policies:

Google Privacy Policy:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

Google AdMob Policy:
https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

3. Children’s Privacy

Lotto Luck is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children.
Because we do not collect data at all, no personal information is stored or shared.

4. No Financial or Gambling Services

Lotto Luck is an entertainment-only tool intended to help users explore number combinations.

We do NOT:

Offer gambling

Facilitate real wagers

Guarantee outcomes

Predict future lottery drawings

All lottery trademarks are owned by their respective entities. Lotto Luck is not affiliated with Powerball® or Multi-State Lottery Association (MUSL).

5. Data Sharing

Since Lotto Luck collects no user information, we do not share or sell any personal data.

The only data processed is handled solely by Google AdMob, as described above.

6. Security

Because no user data is collected or stored, there is no personal information at risk within the app.
